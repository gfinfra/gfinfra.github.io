# Analyst, Project Engineering
GreenFlash is revolutionizing energy storage by optimizing battery systems to power a more sustainable future. Led by power industry veterans [Josh Prueher](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josh-prueher-85416613/), [Ahmad Atwan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmad-atwan-0a4719/), and [Vishal Apte](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vishalapte/), and backed by [H.I.G. Capital](https://higinfrastructure.com/), Greenflash builds, owns, and operates battery energy storage systems (BESS).

We are looking for sharp minds who crave big challenges and want to change the world.

**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
As a Project Engineer, you’ll oversee the technical execution of BESS projects from design to commissioning. You’ll collaborate across teams to solve complex engineering challenges and ensure on-time delivery.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- Bachelor’s in Electrical Engineering (Master’s preferred).
- 3+ years in engineering roles, with 1+ in utility-scale BESS.
- Strong foundation in power systems, controls, and SCADA.
- Experience with AutoCAD, ETAP, or equivalent.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead engineering through design, integration, and commissioning.
- Serve as liaison across finance, operations, and EPC partners.
- Approve system designs, performance models, and vendor submittals.
- Support procurement negotiations and technical decisions.
- Apply lessons learned for continuous improvement.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Solve complex problems across cutting-edge energy projects.
- Gain visibility into executive decision-making.
- Be part of scaling the engineering function of a growth company.
