# Analyst, Electrical Engineer — Data Center
GreenFlash is revolutionizing energy storage by optimizing battery systems to power a more sustainable future. Led by power industry veterans [Josh Prueher](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josh-prueher-85416613/), [Ahmad Atwan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmad-atwan-0a4719/), and [Vishal Apte](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vishalapte/), and backed by [H.I.G. Capital](https://higinfrastructure.com/), Greenflash builds, owns, and operates battery energy storage systems (BESS).

We are looking for sharp minds who crave big challenges and want to change the world.

**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
As an Electrical Engineer, you’ll design and model large-scale data centers with a focus on electrical system stability and grid integration. You’ll apply advanced power systems analysis to ride-through events, islanding, and microgrid controls.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- Bachelor’s in Electrical Engineering (Master’s preferred).
- 2–5 years in power systems, transmission, or load interconnections.
- Skilled with PSS®E, PSLF, PSCAD, ETAP, or DIgSILENT.
- Hands-on exposure to data center electrical infrastructure.
- Familiarity with IEEE, NERC, and interconnection compliance.

## Key Responsibilities
- Perform load flow, dynamic, and transient stability studies.
- Evaluate large electronic loads, UPS, and ride-through behavior.
- Support interconnection with utilities and ISOs/RTOs.
- Review switchgear, transformers, and protection systems.
- Document lessons learned and drive continuous improvement.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Work on cutting-edge large load interconnections.
- Collaborate with leading utilities, developers, and data center operators.
- Apply technical expertise to resilient, next-gen power solutions.
