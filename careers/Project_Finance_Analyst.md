# Project Finance Analyst
**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
Are you a financially savvy thinker with a creative edge? In this role, you’ll sharpen your precision, analytical skills, and communication abilities while supporting our Enterprise Financial Model, capital raising, and M&A initiatives. This is a role where innovation, efficiency, and passion for clean energy directly shape the company’s growth.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Economics, or Finance (CFA a plus).
- Advanced Excel proficiency.
- Highly analytical with strong problem-solving skills.
- Strong communicator able to present complex ideas clearly.
- Experience in energy markets, tax equity, ITCs, or derivatives preferred.
- Passionate about finance and the clean energy transition.

## Key Responsibilities
- Manage and optimize financial analysis of large-scale storage systems.
- Support M&A valuations and due diligence.
- Maintain and enhance enterprise financial models.
- Assist in capital raising and financial structuring.
- Collaborate with internal teams and external advisors.
- Gain hands-on experience in both project and corporate finance.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Build the financial backbone of a rapidly growing clean energy company.
- Exposure to project-level and corporate finance.
- Learn from leaders in project finance, risk management, and energy markets.
- Directly contribute to shaping the energy transition.
