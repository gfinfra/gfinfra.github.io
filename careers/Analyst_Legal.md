## Legal Associate

Location: Pune, India

### Job Description

We want someone who excels in precision, analysis, and communication — a literate, numerate, and articulate legal associate.

You'll be at the intersection of cutting-edge technology and clean energy, managing document workflows and overseeing agreement processes. You will learn to lead and execute all legal agreements needed to finance, build, manage, and operate infrastructure projects in real-time. 

This is not a passive role; it is a role in which you directly contribute to solving some of the world’s most pressing energy challenges.

### Ideal Candidate Profile

- Legal Associate with 3-4 years of relevant experience and a strong foundation in rigorous academic programs.
- Highly numerate and analytical, with the ability to work through complex, data-driven problems.
- Literate and articulate, with strong communication skills to present complex ideas clearly.
- Passionate about clean energy and the opportunity to tackle large-scale, meaningful challenges.

### Key Responsibilities

- **Document Management**: Organize, file, and maintain legal documents, ensuring compliance with filing protocols and secure handling of sensitive information.
- **Review Agreements**: Conduct thorough reviews of business-as-usual (BAU) agreements, including but not limited to non-competes, NDAs, and service agreements, ensuring compliance with legal requirements and company policies.
- **Stakeholder Communication**: Maintain effective communication with internal departments and external partners, ensuring transparency and timely updates throughout the negotiation and agreement review process.
- **Project Manage Negotiations**: Oversee the negotiation process for various agreements by coordinating between internal teams and external stakeholders, tracking timelines, and ensuring milestones are met.
- **Draft and Edit Contracts**: Assist in drafting and editing standard form agreements, ensuring accuracy, clarity, and risk mitigation.
- **Legal Research**: Conduct legal research to support negotiations and contract reviews, identifying relevant regulations and case law to inform decision-making.

### Why GreenFlash?

- Work on projects that are critical to the future of clean energy.
- Hands-on experience with the latest in battery storage and clean energy technology.
- Learn from industry leaders in energy markets, risk management, and data science.
- Make an impact in a company and a sector that is in the earliest stages.
