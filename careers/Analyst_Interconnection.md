# Analyst, Electrical Engineering & Interconnection
GreenFlash is revolutionizing energy storage by optimizing battery systems to power a more sustainable future. Led by power industry veterans [Josh Prueher](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josh-prueher-85416613/), [Ahmad Atwan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmad-atwan-0a4719/), and [Vishal Apte](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vishalapte/), and backed by [H.I.G. Capital](https://higinfrastructure.com/), Greenflash builds, owns, and operates battery energy storage systems (BESS).

We are looking for sharp minds who crave big challenges and want to change the world.

**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
Support the technical execution of utility-scale BESS projects, with a focus on ERCOT interconnection and HV engineering. You’ll be at the heart of transmission planning, equipment design, and regulatory compliance.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- Bachelor’s in Electrical Engineering (Master’s preferred).
- 2–5 years in power systems, transmission, or renewables.
- Familiar with ERCOT interconnection protocols.
- Proficiency in power system modeling tools.

## Key Responsibilities
- Support ERCOT interconnection studies, filings, and deliverables.
- Perform transmission planning and power flow analyses.
- Review substation and HV facility designs.
- Contribute to transformer and breaker procurement evaluations.
- Apply ERCOT, NERC, and IEEE standards.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Hands-on experience with ERCOT and HV project design.
- Build expertise in interconnection and compliance.
- Shape the reliability of large-scale battery systems.
