# Business Development Manager — Data Center Power Solutions
**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
Be our tip-of-spear into the data center market. You’ll build relationships, qualify opportunities, and structure commercial deals for powered shells paired with firm power solutions. Your edge lies in your ability to connect with the data center ecosystem and convert discovery into executable term sheets.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- 3–7 years selling into data centers or mission-critical power.
- Familiarity with data center power basics (UPS, redundancy, SLAs).
- Strong existing network across data center buyers and influencers.
- Clear communicator with strong CRM discipline.
- BESS experience or relationships with colo operators is a plus.

## Key Responsibilities
- Build a qualified deal pipeline and drive pricing iterations.
- Lead discovery with hyperscale/colo teams; document technical needs.
- Partner with engineering to frame deployable solutions.
- Collaborate with QSE/market and finance teams on firming and pricing.
- Lead RFP/RFI responses with compelling proposals.
- Maintain CRM hygiene and competitive intel.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Lead commercial entry into the fast-growing data center sector.
- Work alongside deep internal expertise in BESS and power solutions.
- Shape next-generation firm power solutions for mission-critical customers.
- Competitive salary + commission, with full benefits.
