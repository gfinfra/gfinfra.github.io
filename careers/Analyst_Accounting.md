# Analyst, Accounting
**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
As an Accounting Analyst, you’ll oversee daily accounting for project companies while supporting audits, compliance, and financial reporting. You’ll thrive in a dynamic environment and help build the backbone of GreenFlash’s financial operations.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- Bachelor’s in Accounting or Finance; CPA required.
- 2+ years of accounting experience (public accounting a plus).
- Strong knowledge of GAAP and compliance.
- Highly organized with excellent communication skills.

## Key Responsibilities
- Manage project-level accounting, AP/AR, reconciliations, and close.
- Prepare GAAP-compliant financial statements.
- Conduct variance analyses and budget support.
- Ensure compliance with covenants and financing agreements.
- Support audits, tax filings, and financing diligence.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Exposure to both project-level and corporate accounting.
- Work closely with finance, development, and legal teams.
- Help shape controls and systems from the ground up.
