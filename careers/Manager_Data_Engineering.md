We are looking for sharp minds who crave big challenges and want to change the world.

**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
As a Data Engineer, you’ll build and manage secure cloud data infrastructure—the single source of truth for KPIs and compliance. Your work ensures real-time reporting, security, and reliability for mission-critical operations.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- 3–7 years building cloud data stacks (AWS/Azure/GCP).
- Strong skills in Python/SQL, Terraform/CloudFormation, CI/CD, Git.
- Experience with Kafka/Kinesis, Snowflake/BigQuery, and BI tools.
- Familiarity with IAM, encryption, networking, and DR.

## Key Responsibilities
- Manage ETL pipelines, warehouses, and BI reporting.
- Implement backup/restore SOPs and disaster recovery.
- Enforce encryption, access control, and governance policies.
- Monitor data pipeline health and maintain cloud asset inventory.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Build data systems that power energy operations.
- Exposure to SCADA, time-series, and compliance datasets.
- Competitive salary, bonus eligibility, and full benefits.
