# Analyst, Pre-Construction
GreenFlash is revolutionizing energy storage by optimizing battery systems to power a more sustainable future. Led by power industry veterans [Josh Prueher](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josh-prueher-85416613/), [Ahmad Atwan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmad-atwan-0a4719/), and [Vishal Apte](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vishalapte/), and backed by [H.I.G. Capital](https://higinfrastructure.com/), Greenflash builds, owns, and operates battery energy storage systems (BESS).

We are looking for sharp minds who crave big challenges and want to change the world.

**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
We’re seeking a proactive Pre-Construction Associate to transition late-stage BESS projects into construction. You’ll coordinate permitting, procurement, design, and stakeholder alignment to ensure projects are ready for Notice to Proceed.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- Bachelor’s in engineering, construction management, or related.
- Strong organizational skills with multitasking ability.
- Excellent written and verbal communication.

## Key Responsibilities
- Drive development-to-construction handoff processes.
- Lead readiness reviews for permits, contracts, and designs.
- Coordinate with EPC, engineering, and permitting teams.
- Track procurement, long-lead items, schedules, and budgets.

## Why GreenFlash?
- Direct impact on bringing projects from paper to reality.
- Exposure to development, EPC, and investor stakeholders.
- Competitive salary, bonus, and benefits.
