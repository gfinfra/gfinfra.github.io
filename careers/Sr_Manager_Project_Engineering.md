# Sr. Manager of Project Engineering
**Location:** Houston, TX

## Job Description
As Sr. Manager of Project Engineering, you’ll lead the engineering execution of BESS projects and guide a team from design through commissioning. This is a leadership role with a growth track to Director.

## Ideal Candidate Profile
- Bachelor’s in Electrical Engineering (Master’s preferred).
- 10+ years engineering, 5+ in utility-scale BESS.
- Demonstrated leadership in renewables infrastructure.
- Strong power systems, SCADA, and EMS background.

## Key Responsibilities
- Lead engineering teams from concept through operations.
- Coordinate across utilities, EPCs, OEMs, and internal teams.
- Approve designs, performance models, and interconnection docs.
- Build scalable processes, tools, and templates.
- Influence technical and commercial decisions.

## Why GreenFlash?
- High-impact leadership role with growth to Director.
- Shape execution of utility-scale BESS projects.
- Collaborate across engineering, finance, and operations.
